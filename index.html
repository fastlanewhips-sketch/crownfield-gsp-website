Know the breed

German Shorthaired Pointer Traits

Important things to know before bringing a GSP home.

Active & Athletic

GSPs are energetic sporting dogs that need daily exercise, outdoor activities, and mental stimulation.

Intelligent & Trainable

They often learn quickly and respond well to consistent, reward-based training and clear routines.

People-Oriented

Many GSPs enjoy being close to their people and do best when included in family life.

A thoughtful start

Preparing for Puppy Ownership

Welcoming a puppy is a long-term commitment. Prepare for the needs of an active sporting breed.

Home & Routine

Prepare a safe space, establish a predictable schedule, and plan for supervision, rest, and potty breaks.

Veterinary Care

Arrange a veterinary visit and discuss vaccinations, parasite prevention, nutrition, and preventive care.

Training & Enrichment

Use gentle, reward-based training, age-appropriate play, sniffing games, and safe socialization.
