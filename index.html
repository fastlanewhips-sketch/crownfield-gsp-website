Welcome to Crown Field

A remarkable breed with a remarkable spirit.

German Shorthaired Pointers are admired for their versatility, intelligence, athletic ability, and affectionate personalities.

Their energetic nature and eagerness to participate in family activities make them especially appealing to people who enjoy outdoor adventures and an active lifestyle.

At Crown Field, we welcome thoughtful inquiries from individuals and families interested in learning more about this distinctive breed.

What makes German Shorthaired Pointers special?

Learn about the qualities, personality, and lifestyle needs that make German Shorthaired Pointers unique.

Intelligence

German Shorthaired Pointers are intelligent dogs that benefit from consistent training, mental stimulation, and positive reinforcement.

Athleticism

These energetic dogs thrive with regular exercise, outdoor activities, and opportunities to explore their surroundings.

Companionship

With appropriate training and socialization, German Shorthaired Pointers can become devoted companions and active family members.
