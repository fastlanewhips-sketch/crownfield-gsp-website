Welcome to Crown Field

A lifelong companion deserves a thoughtful beginning.

German Shorthaired Pointers are energetic, intelligent, versatile dogs known for their devotion and enthusiasm. They thrive with active families who can provide daily exercise, training, enrichment, and companionship.

At Crown Field, our goal is to help prospective owners understand the breed and make an informed, responsible decision. Please contact us for current details, health documentation, and availability.

  • Discuss your home, lifestyle, and experience
