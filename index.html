The German Shorthaired Pointer

A versatile sporting breed known for its athleticism, intelligence, enthusiasm and affectionate nature.

Intelligence & Trainability

German Shorthaired Pointers are intelligent dogs that benefit from positive training, consistency and engaging activities.

Energy & Adventure

This active breed enjoys exercise, outdoor adventures and opportunities to explore. Daily physical and mental activity is important.

Affection & Companionship

A Thoughtful Decision

Preparing for a German Shorthaired Pointer

Welcoming a dog into your home is a long-term commitment. Understanding the breed's needs helps create a positive experience for both you and your companion.

Exercise & Enrichment

German Shorthaired Pointers need regular exercise, mental stimulation and opportunities to engage in appropriate activities.

Training & Socialization

Consistent, positive training and early socialization help puppies develop confidence and good everyday habits.

Veterinary Care

Routine veterinary visits, preventive care, appropriate nutrition and health monitoring are essential throughout a dog's life.

Long-Term Commitment

Consider your schedule, living environment, activity level and ability to provide lifelong care before welcoming a dog.

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful Information

Find answers to common questions about German Shorthaired Pointers and contacting Crown Field.

What is a German Shorthaired Pointer?

The German Shorthaired Pointer is a versatile sporting dog breed recognized for its intelligence, athleticism, distinctive coat and affectionate nature.

Are German Shorthaired Pointers energetic?

Yes. They are generally an active breed that benefits from regular exercise, training, enrichment and opportunities to explore.

How can I inquire about puppy availability?

Contact Crown Field using the phone number provided on this website to ask questions about availability and the next steps.

What should I ask before welcoming a puppy?

Ask about veterinary records, health history, vaccinations, socialization, parent information, contracts and any applicable health guarantees. Review all documentation carefully.
